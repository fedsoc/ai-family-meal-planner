DayBento: Meal Planner keeps track of the food in your fridge, freezer and pantry, shows you what to use first, and suggests recipes from what you already have.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad

Free for the first month, then $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year.

Everything in its place

Less food in the bin, fewer “what’s for dinner?” moments, and a shopping list that knows what’s at home.

Snap the receipt. Done.

Take a photo of a grocery receipt, scan a barcode or type an item. About 150,000 US grocery products are recognized on your device, and you review every line before it’s added.

Use it before you lose it

See what to use first. Every date says where it came from: the package, or a USDA FoodKeeper estimate, always labeled as one.

Cook with what you have

Recipes ranked by what’s in your kitchen, showing what you have and what’s missing.

Plan the week

Put meals on the days ahead, save leftovers and write your own recipes.

A list that knows your kitchen

Missing recipe ingredients go on the list for you, sorted by aisle. What you buy goes back into the kitchen.

Private by design

  • No account. Your kitchen stays on your device.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Export it as JSON or CSV, or erase it all, any time.

One subscription, the whole app

Start with a free month. Works with VoiceOver and the largest text sizes, and Family Sharing is supported.

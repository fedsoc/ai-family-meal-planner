Support

Big Beautiful Bowl

Contact

Email bigbeautifulbowl.support@icloud.com, or in the app choose Help → Send Feedback. We read every message. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. No account is needed. The AI features need a device with Apple Intelligence; everything they do also has a manual way.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account. Most of the app works offline. It goes online only when you use a feature that needs it: finding nearby stores, importing a recipe from a web page, searching for recipes or product information online, downloading recipe packs, and subscribing.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your device only; we never receive it. Settings → Your Data → Erase All Data removes it, and deleting the app removes it too. See the Privacy Policy.

What does the subscription include?

Big Beautiful Bowl is a monthly or yearly subscription, with a free one-month trial for new subscribers (once per Apple Account). It includes the whole app: kitchen tracking, meal planning, shopping lists and recipe packs. If your subscription ends, you can still view your kitchen, shopping list and planned meals; nothing is deleted, and subscribing again restores everything.

How do I cancel, restore or change my subscription?

Go to Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription in the app, or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. After reinstalling or on a new device, tap Restore Purchases in Settings → Subscription. Apple handles payment, billing and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can my family use my subscription?

Yes, with Family Sharing. Turn on Family Sharing in your Apple Account settings, and up to five family members can use it on their own devices.

What are recipe packs?

Extra recipe collections, added regularly and included in the subscription. Find them in Meals → More Recipes. Downloaded packs stay on your device. To hear about new ones, turn on New Recipe Notifications in Settings.

A receipt scan missed or misread a line.

Every scan opens a review screen before anything is saved, so you can fix or add lines there. Clear, flat, well-lit photos read best.

A barcode isn't found.

Barcodes are matched against a product list built into the app. If a product isn't in it, add the item by name; the app remembers that barcode for next time.

Why does an item say “unknown amount”?

The app never guesses an amount. If a receipt or label didn't say how much, the amount stays unknown until you enter it.

How do I share a recipe or my shopping list?

Open the recipe and tap Share Recipe, or tap Share List on the shopping list. A recipe imported from the web is shared as a link to its original page.

Are the recipes tested?

Each recipe shows its source and is reviewed before release. Always cook meat, poultry, fish and eggs to the safe temperatures the recipe gives.

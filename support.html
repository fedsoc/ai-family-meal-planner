Support

DayBento: Meal Planner

Contact

Email aifamilymealplanner-support@icloud.com, or in the app choose Help → Send Feedback. We read every message; please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 26 or later. No account is needed.

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Everything works on your device. The internet is used only when you ask for it: finding nearby stores, importing a recipe from a web page, or opening a recipe search.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your device only. Settings → Your Data → Erase All Data removes it; deleting the app removes it too. We never receive it. See the privacy policy.

Why does an item show "about" or "unknown amount"?

The app never guesses an amount. If a receipt or label didn't say how much, it shows the amount as unknown until you enter it.

A receipt scan missed or misread a line.

Every scan opens a review screen before anything is saved: fix or add lines there. Clear, flat, well-lit photos read best.

A barcode isn't found.

Barcodes are matched against a product list that ships inside the app. If a product isn't in it, add the item by name; the app remembers that barcode for next time.

Are the AI features available?

AI features run only on your device with Apple Intelligence, where your device supports it and Apple Intelligence is turned on. Everything they do also has a manual way in the app.

How do I share a recipe or my shopping list?

Open the recipe and tap Share Recipe, or tap Share List on the Shopping list. A recipe imported from the web is shared as a link to its original page.

Are the recipes tested?

Each library recipe shows its source and has been reviewed before release. Always cook meat, poultry, fish and eggs to the safe temperatures the recipe gives.

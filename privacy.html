Privacy Policy

DayBento: Meal Planner · Effective November 16, 2026

DayBento Meal Planner helps you keep track of the food in your kitchen, plan meals and shop. It works on your device, without an account.

What we collect

Nothing. DayBento Meal Planner has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We (the developer) do not receive your kitchen items, shopping lists, recipes, receipts, photos, barcodes or settings.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter is stored in a database on your iPhone or iPad. If you delete the app, or use Settings → Your Data → Erase All Data, it is removed from your device. Receipt photos are read on your device and are not kept unless you choose to keep them. Barcodes are looked up in a product list that ships inside the app; scanning one sends nothing anywhere.

Apple Intelligence

AI features use Apple's on-device model, where your device supports it and the feature is turned on. Your requests are processed on your device, not sent to us or to any server.

When the app connects to the internet

Only when you tap something that needs it:

  • Find nearby grocery stores: the app asks Apple Maps for grocery stores near you. Your location is asked for only when you tap, used for that one search, and never stored or sent to us. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple Maps.
  • Import a recipe from a web page: the app downloads the one page whose address you enter or share, to read the recipe from it. The website sees a normal page request from your device, as it would in a browser. The recipe is saved on your device with a link to its source.
  • Finding recipes online (if you turn it on): the search you review opens in an in-app Safari view; Safari's browsing is separate from the app and we can't see it.
  • Translate a recipe to English: translation runs on your device with Apple Translation. If a language isn't on your device yet, iOS asks before downloading it from Apple.

Sharing

When you use Share Recipe or Share List, the app hands the text (or, for an imported recipe, its web link) to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you send it, such as Messages or Mail.

The app does not send your data to any other company.

Feedback and diagnostics

If you choose Help → Send Feedback, your email app opens with a message to daybento-support@icloud.com. You decide what to send. You can attach a diagnostic report; it contains only app and system version, device model and counts — no item names, text, photos or AI requests.

Children

DayBento Meal Planner is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Privacy Policy

DayBento: Meal Planner · Effective November 26, 2026

DayBento: Meal Planner (“the app”) helps you keep track of the food in your kitchen, plan meals and shop. It is published by Day Bento (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app, our website and our support email, and the rights you have under the privacy laws of the United States (including California and the other U.S. states with privacy laws), Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union.

The short version

  • The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • What you enter in the app is stored on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive it.
  • The AI features run on your device with Apple Intelligence. Nothing you ask them is sent to us or to a server.
  • The app goes online only for features you use, such as finding nearby stores or importing a recipe from a web page.
  • The only personal information we receive is what you send us when you email us.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we never will.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you enter or create in the app is stored in a database on your device and is not sent to us. This includes your kitchen items and their amounts and dates, shopping lists, meal plans, recipes and notes, cooking history, household settings (such as servings and units), and any allergies or foods you choose to avoid.

  • Receipt and product photos are read on your device. A product photo is never uploaded or kept. A receipt photo is not kept unless you choose to keep it.
  • Barcodes are looked up in a product list that ships inside the app. Scanning a barcode sends nothing anywhere.
  • Spotlight and widgets: if you use them, the app puts recipe titles (and, if you turn it on, kitchen items) in your device's Spotlight search index, and shows items in its widgets. Both stay on your device.

You can remove all of it at any time with Settings → Your Data → Erase All Data. Deleting the app removes it too. Your device's own backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include the app's data, as for any app; those backups are managed by you and Apple, not by us.

Permissions the app asks for

Each one is optional and is asked for only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change it at any time in your device's Settings.

  • Camera and photos: to scan barcodes and read receipts and product labels, on your device.
  • Location: only when you tap to find nearby grocery stores. It is used for that one search and is not stored or sent to us.
  • Notifications: off unless you turn them on. The alerts (such as “a new recipe pack is ready”) are created on your device. We run no push server and receive no device token.

Apple Intelligence

Where your device supports Apple Intelligence and a feature is turned on, the app uses Apple's on-device model. Your requests and their results are processed on your device. They are not sent to us, to Apple's Private Cloud Compute or to any other server. Every AI feature has a manual way to do the same thing, and you can turn each one off in Settings.

When the app connects to the internet

Only for the features below, and only when you use them:

  • Find nearby grocery stores: the app asks Apple Maps for stores near your location. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to Apple Maps.
  • Import a recipe from a web page: the app downloads the one page whose address you enter or share. The website sees a normal page request from your device, as it would in a browser.
  • Find recipes or research a product online (off unless you turn them on): the search you review and can edit first opens your chosen search engine in an in-app Safari view. If you ask the app to summarize a product page, it downloads that one page and summarizes it on your device. The search engine and the websites you visit see your request as they would in Safari, and their own privacy policies apply. The app does not send your kitchen data or the barcode unless it is in the search you reviewed.
  • Translate a recipe: translation runs on your device with Apple Translation. If a language isn't on your device yet, iOS asks before downloading it from Apple.
  • Recipe packs (where offered): the app checks Apple's iCloud public database for new recipe packs when you open it, now and then in the background, and when you tap Download. You don't need to sign in to iCloud, and nothing about you or your kitchen is sent. Every copy of the app makes the same request.
  • Subscription: Apple handles purchases, payment and your subscription, and Apple's Privacy Policy applies. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Through Apple, the app only learns whether there is an active subscription or trial.

Like any internet request, these requests reveal your device's IP address to the service that receives them (Apple, the website or the search engine). We don't receive it.

Information we receive: when you contact us

If you email us, or use Help → Send Feedback in the app (which opens your own email app), we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. You can attach a diagnostic report; it contains only the app, system and database versions, the device model, counts, and the codes and times of recent errors (for example “network timeout at 14:02”), never item names, text, photos or AI requests. You decide what to send.

  • Why we use it: to answer you, fix problems and improve the app.
  • Legal basis (EU and UK): our legitimate interest in answering and supporting the people who use the app, and taking steps you ask us to take.
  • Who processes it: our email provider, Apple (iCloud Mail), stores it for us as a service provider.
  • How long we keep it: for as long as we need it to help you, and no longer than 24 months after your last message. Then we delete it.

Visiting this website: it is hosted on GitHub Pages. We use no cookies, analytics or trackers here. GitHub may log your IP address for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement; we don't receive those logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we use no advertising or analytics tools.
  • We don't build profiles about you or make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.
  • We don't collect sensitive personal information or consumer health data. Allergies and foods you avoid stay on your device.
  • We don't disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing.

Because there is nothing to opt out of, the app and this website have no “Do Not Sell or Share” setting. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we treat it as a request not to sell or share, which we already don't do.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we have about you, to get a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw any consent you gave. You won't be treated differently for using any of these rights.

The information in the app is already in your hands: you can see, change and delete it in the app, and Erase All Data removes all of it. For the emails you have sent us, write to daybento.support@icloud.com. We will check the request comes from you by replying to the address you wrote from, and we will answer within 30 days (or sooner if your local law requires).

United States

This section applies to residents of California and of the other states with consumer privacy laws, such as Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia.

  • What we collected in the last 12 months: only if you emailed us, identifiers (your email address) and the content of your messages, received from you, used only for the purposes above and disclosed only to our email provider as a service provider. We did not sell or share any personal information, including of anyone under 16, and we collect no sensitive personal information.
  • Your rights: to know or access, correct, delete, get a portable copy, and opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling (none of which we do). You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that you gave the agent permission.

  • Health data laws (such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's similar law): we do not collect, share or sell consumer health data.

European Union, European Economic Area and United Kingdom

Day Bento is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. In addition to the rights above, you have the right to data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can complain to your local data protection authority, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to help first.

Where personal information leaves the EU, EEA or UK, we rely on the protections the law provides for it, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Canada

We handle personal information in line with PIPEDA and the provincial laws that apply, including Québec's Law 25. By emailing us, you agree to us using your information to answer you as described above. You can ask to access or correct it, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Your emails are stored in the United States and may be accessed there under U.S. law. The person responsible for privacy at Day Bento can be reached at daybento.support@icloud.com. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Security

The app's data is protected by your device's encryption and passcode. The app downloads pages and recipe packs only over HTTPS, and every recipe pack is checked against our signature before it is saved. No method is perfectly secure, but keeping your data on your device means there is no copy of it on our servers to be breached.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). If you believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete the message.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change affects how we handle information we already have, we will tell you in the app or on this page before it applies.
